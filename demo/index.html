How equal positions connect

A teaching example. It is not the solver and shares none of its code.

What you are seeing

A shift ties each position to the one a fixed distance away. Tied positions must hold the same letter, so they end up in one group.

You can find the groups by checking every tie, or with a shortcut that settles a whole stretch at once and then works on a smaller piece.
